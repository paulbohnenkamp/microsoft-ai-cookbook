# Fictional enterprise: Northstar

Northstar is a fictional mid-sized services company using Microsoft 365, Power Platform, Azure, and increasingly Microsoft AI capabilities to build internal business solutions.

The company has six departments: Human Resources, Finance and Accounting, Marketing, Sales, Operations, and Information Technology. At a high level, it has employees, customers, service requests, invoices, documents, policies, contracts, and approvals.

Use Northstar only to give recipes a consistent business context. Do not expand it into a complete domain model, organizational chart, company history, or enterprise architecture. A recipe may introduce a small additional detail when the example needs one. See [`cookbook-environment.md`](cookbook-environment.md) for the platform boundaries and progressive setup model.
